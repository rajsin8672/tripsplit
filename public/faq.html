Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about TripSplit's foundation, features, and settlements.

What is TripSplit?

TripSplit is a specialized travel expense-sharing application built for groups of friends. It tracks shared expenses (accommodations, dining, fuel, tickets), calculates who owes whom, and simplifies settlements using UPI payments.

Does TripSplit charge transaction fees or hold funds?

No. TripSplit never touches your money or operates as a financial intermediary. All payments are executed peer-to-peer using your installed UPI apps (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM) directly to the recipient's verified UPI ID.

What features are available right now in Phase 1?

Phase 1 implements the complete core technical foundation: real Firebase Email/Password authentication, persistent sessions, password reset flows, secure token verification middleware on Node.js/Express, and Firestore user profiles with unique User Codes and UPI IDs.

When will trip creation and expense splitting be live?

Trip management, friend invitations, split algorithms, and real-time ledger updates are scheduled for Phase 2. Phase 3 will introduce automated UPI deep linking and settlement confirmations.

How do I reset my account password?

Click "Forgot password?" on the login page, enter your registered email address, and an official password reset link will be sent to your inbox by Firebase Authentication.

Need support or have feedback?

TripSplit is an open project in active development. For technical inquiries, bug reports, or setup questions, please consult the project repository documentation.
