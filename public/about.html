About Us

Why We Built TripSplit

Group travel is one of life's best experiences, but calculating who paid for what and dealing with spreadsheets at the end of a vacation is stressful. TripSplit was created to make financial accountability clear, simple, and direct.

Our Core Principles

1. Radical Transparency

Every member of a trip sees exactly who paid for each expense, the time it was logged, and the mathematical formula used to split the debt.

2. Debt Simplification

Instead of five friends paying each other back and forth in multiple transactions, our debt-minimizing graph algorithm computes the minimum number of settlements required.

3. Direct UPI Settlement

TripSplit does not hold your money or act as a digital wallet. Debts are settled directly between friends using standard UPI apps like Google Pay, PhonePe, and Paytm.

Phase 1 Foundation

This application is currently in Phase 1: establishing robust Firebase authentication, authenticated API synchronization, and user profiles with UPI ID configuration.

Trip creation, expense ledgers, and multi-currency calculations will be unlocked in Phase 2, followed by advanced settlement automation in Phase 3.
